# FF8 Clippers Phase 1 — Wiki

This wiki is ingested from the raw dataset in [`raw/`](../raw), specifically
`FF8 clippers phase 1 data.xlsx`. It documents a Phase 1 influencer/creator
seeding campaign for the Samsung Galaxy **Z Fold8 / Z Fold8 Ultra / Z Flip8**
launch in Indonesia.

- [Campaign Overview](Campaign-Overview.md) — what the campaign is, schema, data quality notes
- [Performance Breakdown](Performance-Breakdown.md) — daily, platform, approach, content type, and product stats
- [Creator Leaderboard](Creator-Leaderboard.md) — top creators and top posts by views
